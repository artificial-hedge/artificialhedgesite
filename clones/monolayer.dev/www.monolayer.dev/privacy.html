Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Monolayer respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you use our website or product:

  • Basic account information, such as your name, email address, and company name.
  • Product usage information, such as pages visited, actions taken, and general interaction data.
  • Technical information, such as browser type, device type, operating system, and approximate location.
  • Payment and billing information, handled by our payment provider.

We do not collect more information than we need to provide, improve, and secure Monolayer.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use cookies on our website.

We use PostHog to understand how people use Monolayer and improve the product. PostHog is configured to collect analytics without using cookies or persistent browser storage.

This helps us understand things like which pages are visited, which features are used, and where users may run into issues.

Why we use your information

We use your information to:

  • provide and operate Monolayer;
  • create and manage user accounts;
  • process payments;
  • improve the website and product;
  • understand product usage;
  • provide support;
  • keep Monolayer secure;
  • communicate important updates.

Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party services to operate Monolayer, including services for:

  • analytics;
  • payments;
  • hosting and infrastructure;
  • customer support;
  • email communication.

These services only receive the information needed to perform their role.

Your cloud and your data

Monolayer is designed to run infrastructure in your own cloud account.

We do not claim ownership of your code, applications, or infrastructure. You remain responsible for the data and systems inside your own cloud environment.

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide Monolayer, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve the product.

You can request deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to certain uses of your information;
  • request a copy of your information.

To make a request, contact us at:

[privacy@monolayer.dev]

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

No system is completely secure, but we work to keep Monolayer protected.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

privacy@monolayer.dev
